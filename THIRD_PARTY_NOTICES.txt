Download tools
--------------
The macOS installers do not include yt-dlp, FFmpeg or FFprobe executables.
Grabber downloads them directly from upstream when the user installs tools.
Their licenses are separate from Grabber's MIT license.

yt-dlp source is published under the Unlicense:
https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp
https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/blob/master/LICENSE
PyInstaller executables incorporate GPLv3+ and other third-party code:
https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/blob/master/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt
https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/releases

FFmpeg and FFprobe licensing depends on the configuration of the particular build.
https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html
Grabber's tool provider publishes binaries, license files and build information:
https://github.com/eugeneware/ffmpeg-static/releases
The macOS arm64 build currently reports --enable-nonfree. FFmpeg marks such
builds as non-redistributable, so it is not bundled in Grabber's macOS release.
Run ffmpeg -L and ffmpeg -version to inspect a downloaded build's license and flags.

If you distribute a build that includes downloaded tools (including Windows
installers), you are responsible for retaining their notices and satisfying
the licenses of those exact binaries and their dependencies, including any
applicable corresponding-source requirements.
